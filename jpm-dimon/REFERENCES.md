# Reference breakdowns (watched frame by frame)

**Method.** Each reference was split into dense contact sheets: the first 15 s at 15 fps, the key beats at full resolution, and the body at one frame every 2–3.5 s. Each was transcribed locally with Parakeet. The notes below are what was lifted into v4, and where.

## Hemant Taneja reel (RC Media 10.4)

### 0–1.7 s: "This man is the modern day…"
**On screen:**
- He sits on a dark stage. For most frames he is a flat cream silhouette with a soft glow; his live picture shows through for one frame, or a few, every 5–8 frames.
- The silhouette starts pale grey, then turns bright cream at about 0.7 s.

**Type and effects:**
- Serif words sit left and right of his head: "This" small, "man" big and "is the" small on the left; "modern" with a small "day" on the right.
- White glitch bars flash around the words, and a white band slices through "modern".

**Cut:** a hard cut to black, where photo cards pop around the centre name. A white box marks each card's spot one or two frames before it lands.

### 22–25.5 s
**On screen:** his stage video on a blurred backdrop, with "General Catalyst" in sans on his chest.

**Effects:** white boxes (and once a red one) flash, then picture cards land to his left and right at different heights. Small text lines build across his chest.

**Why it works:** the silhouette turns a talking head into an icon before we see his face. The boxes give every card an anticipation frame, so nothing just appears.

**Lifted into v4:**
- **S02, 9.05–10.68:** the stage freeze, the opaque grey-then-cream silhouette, live frames at six points, "This is the most" on the left, "influential" on the right, white bars, and the band through "influential".
- **S02, 11.08–12.36:** the interview pauses, he comes forward with a glow, and the seal rises behind his head. Five photo cards land around him, each after a white box. "right now" is in sans.
- **S01, 8.62:** the same glow cut-out pops up after JPMORGAN CHASE.

## Dario Amodei reel (RC Media 11.3)

### 16.6–17.6 s: SUDDENLY
**Before the word:** two frames of small white squares at the corners of the outgoing shot, then one glitch frame with the word in black on a white band.

**The word:** white uppercase serif at the centre with a tiny copy under its right end. Giant blurred echoes are cropped at the top and bottom of the frame. It holds about 1 s.

### 36.6–37.4 s: DISAPPEAR
**The word:**
- Typed letter by letter at about 0.055 s a letter, over continuous fire footage.
- The newest letter blooms; earlier letters settle crisp with a soft glow.

**Exit and surroundings:** the word leaves through glitch blocks. Small serif words sit on either side before and after.

**Why it works:** the word is simply there, with no fly-in. The suddenness comes from the corner squares and one inverted frame, not from motion.

**Lifted into v4:**

- **S01, 7.28–8.45 "JPMORGAN CHASE":** typed to the reporter's syllables over the J.P.Morgan flag, then the JPMorgan tower at dusk. It leaves through glitch blocks, and the tower holds for a beat.
