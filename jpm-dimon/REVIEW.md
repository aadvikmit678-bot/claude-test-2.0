# Jamie Dimon / JPMorgan Chase — review (v2)

## What changed from v1
The user's verdict on v1: pictures held for 2–3 s with a slow zoom and text in front; no motion graphics and no real
animation. v2 is rebuilt from scratch in `tools/build2.py`.

**Pace and sources**
- 134 cut-down shots and 73 stills, drawn from every Drive source:
  - the Bloomberg and CNBC pieces, the career documentary, the "Untold" documentary, the 60 Minutes 2008 stories;
  - the Stanford and Atlantic interviews, the Pinterest clips, the image pack, and cut-outs of Dimon and Bezos.
- A new picture lands every 1–3 words, as in the Fink reel.

**Set pieces**
- "BIG": footage seen through a giant word, then the camera dives through the letter.
- "BIGGER" and "COMEBACK": the Dario SUDDENLY echo.
- "the most influential man": the seal ring, with Dimon portraits swapping every 3 frames, then a glint, rays and a white frame.
- "man in America": the seal portrait with negative frames, then a hard punch-in on "America".
- "Wall Street": face-detection boxes on the Wolf.
- "1998", "16 years", "2004", "2008": year rollers with floating photo cards.
- "over a bonus": a 2×2 money grid.
- "Meet Jamie Dimon": the Fink name card. A cut-out on a card settles on paper, and the name decodes letter by letter
  under a giant outline echo.
- "next in line … the biggest financial company": a red marker sweeps the real 1998 NYT clipping ("Heir Apparent",
  then "the world's largest financial services company").
- "side by side": a split screen.
- "But then" and "he was": the bar write-on.
- "fired": the Strange palm strike with a white flash and negative frames.
- "18 months": a counter, then a window zoom-out on the tunnel.
- "boxing": a style strobe in six print looks with a punch-in.
- "Amazon": the word glows behind a Bezos cut-out (the Hemant reel).
- "the history of Wall Street": a 24-tile archival photo wall.
- "In 2000": typed giant digits.
- "broken": a glitch.
- "cuts the dividend": a diagonal slice.
- "cleaned it up": a dirty-to-clean wipe.
- "$60 million": a chunk counter.
- "own money": the porthole.
- "$500M loss into billions": the AMD cream data page.
- "the ones that fell": the Lehman and WaMu logos drop.
- "JP Morgan & Chase": a light-swept wordmark.
- "$10 trillion": a counter over a screen wall and falling money.
- "Today": typed.

**Typography:** the Pattinson engine.
- Instrument Serif glow with the white flicker box on line words, and a blown-out bloom on hero words.
- Sans words glow in.
- Every line slides to stay centred as each word arrives.
- Black serif on lit paper ("game").

## Measured vs looked at
- **Measured:** `check` (lint, runtime, layout, contrast, craft).
- **Looked at:** every beat in about 150 snapshot frames, plus re-shoots of every fix.
- **Removed from screen:** channel bugs, burned-in subtitles and labels, cropped out of the AT&T, 270 Park and
  YouTuber shots.
- **Audio:** the audio is the v1 mix (news, VO, Dimon interview, at −16 LUFS). It was measured, not heard.
