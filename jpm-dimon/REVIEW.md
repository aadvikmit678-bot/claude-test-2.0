# Jamie Dimon / JPMorgan Chase — review (v1)

## What this is
A 93.2 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps documentary short.
- **Opening:** the Bloomberg news line, trimmed to "The name of the game in American financial capitalism is being big,
  and no one is bigger on Wall Street than JPMorgan Chase."
- **Narration:** the user's voiceover follows.
- **Dimon in his own words:** the narrator's paraphrase is replaced by the real Bloomberg interview ("We bank some of
  these companies in 30 countries. We bank countries. We bank the World Bank, the IMF. We bank the United States
  government. You have to be big. You can't do those things and be small.").
- **Rest of the narration**, ending on "$10 trillion every single day today".

The build is generated by `tools/build.py` from `tools/prep_media.py` assets. Re-run both after any change.

## Creative calls
- **Format:** 16:9 (the user switched from 9:16). The two vertical sources (money porthole, chess) sit as centred strips
  on a black halftone ground.
- **Captions:** the Dimon-reel system.
  - Satoshi Medium 62 px for the small line, Satoshi Bold 150 px for the key noun, Instrument Serif Italic 172 px for
    the emotive word.
  - Warm white #FBF8EC with a soft bloom.
  - One word at a time on its spoken onset (Parakeet word times, snapped to the waveform).
  - Each word comes in with a 3-frame horizontal motion blur (SVG feGaussianBlur "X 0") and leaves on a cascading fade.
    No pop, no bounce, no slide.
  - The reference reel's own lockups are rebuilt on Dimon's interview: "some / these", "30 / Countries",
    "We bank / IMF", "You have to be / big" vs a tiny "small".
- **Scrims:** a soft radial scrim is added automatically behind a caption only where the measured picture behind it is
  bright (28 groups).
- **Look:**
  - Every scene carries the user's halftone prints: Halftone Grunge (overlay 42 %) and Halftone 6 (soft light 30 %).
  - Footage gets one warm documentary grade with faint scanlines and chromatic fringe.
  - The 2004 Fox and 2008 news inserts and the Fire Sale report use the CRT Display recipe (the old-TV texture).
  - Archival photos are B&W; young Dimon gets the gold bloom grade.
- **B-roll:** cut on the spoken noun, from every source in the Drive.
  - Doctor Strange palm strike lands exactly on "fired" with a white flash, then the astral fall.
  - Bank One tower glitches on "broken"; the dividend is sliced; a dirty copy wipes clean on "cleaned it up".
  - A cream data page (AMD reference) draws Bank One net income from -$511M (2000) to +$3.5B (2003) — real figures.
  - 2008: the Fox "DOW FALLS 733 POINTS" broadcast, the NYSE floor, fire, Lehman, a 1930s bank run, "Your bank has
    failed" (FDIC), the falling chess pieces with the king standing.
  - Then the "Last Man Standing" book, the Fed money helicopter, the Fire Sale / Bear Stearns reports, the JPMorgan
    Chase signs and the NYSE JPM board.
- **Excluded:** the Larry Fink clip (the user said it is for someone else). No sound effects (the user's standing
  preference).

## Measured vs looked at
- **Measured:** `check` passes with 0 errors.
- **Looked at:** 90+ snapshot frames across the whole film, and every caption placement against on-screen text
  (chyrons, signs).
- **Audio:** the audio mix was not heard. The three pieces (news, VO, interview) are loudness-normalised to -16 LUFS,
  and the joins sit in measured silences.

## Warnings kept
- `line_unread` on "+$3.5B profit": the checker reads it as the film's last line. It holds 1.3 s on the data page and
  the narration says it.
- `composition_file_too_large` and `timeline_track_too_dense` on captions.html: all captions sit in one captions host,
  as the captions reference asks.
- `duplicate_media_discovery_risk`: the clean and dirty copies of the "cleaned it up" shot.
