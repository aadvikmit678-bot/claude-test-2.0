## The recipes (at 1920x1080)
1. **Cadence.**
   - Photos hold dead still, with no push or drift (template matching gives scale 1.000 and 0 px on every hold).
   - The speed comes from the cuts: 2-7 frames a photo in the name run, 4-6 frames under the words.
   - Single black frames sit between devices (f2, f7, f31, f33, f86, f151).
2. **Name flank.** Two words at eye level, the face between them:
   - left word right-aligned to x 473, right word left-aligned at x 1401, vertical centre y 543;
   - white sans, cap height ~75 px (about 100 px Inter);
   - the words stay put while the photos change under them.
3. **Negative rectangle on the face.**
   - A 960x539 box (half the frame) centred on the face, shown for 2 frames on a photo.
   - Inside: the picture inverted (202 - v).
   - It stays at the same place across a cut to the next photo.
   - Black frames before and after.
4. **Blur-in.** A new photo arrives for 1 frame fully defocused and 4-10 % small, words included; sharp on the next frame.
5. **Vertical whip.** 2 frames:
   - first the outgoing photo smeared vertically;
   - then a pure grey vertical smear;
   - then a hard cut to the next picture, already settled.
6. **Coloured word fly-in.**
   - A blurred blob of colour starts left of centre and slides to the centre.
   - It resolves to a sharp bold word over 5 frames.
   - Red #BD2236 in the reel; ours is gold, for "golden boy".
7. **Top negative rectangle.**
   - 1 frame of a 671x581 negative box flush with the top edge, centred.
   - Black frames on both sides (a strobe).
8. **Silhouette frame.** 1 frame of the subject as a flat light-grey matte (#B1B1B1) on the dark picture.
9. **Negative entry.** The next shot's first frame is a full negative.
10. **White bar -> word.**
    - A solid white bar the size of the coming word, 2-3 frames.
    - Then the word itself, sharp, in the same box.
    - At the end of a phrase the words go back to bars (1-2 frames).
11. **Focus window.**
    - 3 frames of a 1084x605 box (x 346-1430, y 259-864).
    - Inside: sharp and inverted, with the white word turning black.
    - Outside: blurred, dimmed to 50 %.
    - Then 1 black frame, then the clean picture.
12. **Newspaper.**
    - A still front page, framed full.
    - A white bar on the headline turns into a word.
    - The paper's photos go negative for 2 frames.
13. **Map pan.**
    - The picture moves steadily, about 1 % zoom-out a frame, with motion blur.
    - The words come in one by one from bars; the last word is in bold caps.
14. **Strobe.** White, black, white, white (4 frames), before a new photo.
15. **Punch-in.** +7.5 %, +6.5 %, +2 % over 3 frames, then hold.
