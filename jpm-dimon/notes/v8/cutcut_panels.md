## How it is built
- The panels are the panes of the building's glass. Each pane opens onto the next shot, so the transition reads as looking out through the windows.
- One panel arrives every 4-8 frames, left to right (a "cut, cut, cut" rhythm).
- Each panel is its own crop of the aerial, a few percent offset in position and scale from its neighbours. The view looks fractured across the seams, not like one picture under a mask.
- The seams are thin vertical lines (about 6-8 px at 1080), dark.
- About 1.4 s from the first panel to the clean cut. The aerial keeps moving (drone push) inside the panels.
