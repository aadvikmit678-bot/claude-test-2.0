# The Alex Karp reel (instagram Dfc3GMjxrP5): the editing for everything after "Meet Jamie Dimon"

1916x1080, 24 fps, 88.9 s. Read at 2 fps over the whole reel and frame by frame from 14 to 25 s, where the user said to start ("the first sort of animation").

## What the reel does
- **Video first.**
  - Almost every beat is a moving clip, 0.5-1.5 s, cut on the phrase.
  - The clip shows what the words say: "stock went" is a trading floor, "six dollars" a trader on the phone, "Goldman Sachs" the Goldman sign, "CIA" the control room.
- **Black beats.** A few small words alone on black between phrases: "he wasn't", "cut out for", "And then", "so", "that".
- **The pinboard (14.75-19.5 s).**
  - A talking clip blows out for 4 frames (a posterize/threshold flash) and shrinks into a card.
  - The camera whips across a pale board to the next card, a video with a white edge pinned with a red pushpin and slightly turned. The words stack beside it.
  - The camera pans down to a third card and pushes in.
- **Cards over a blurred background.** A photo with a white edge floating over a blurred black-and-white shot (21.5 s), or on black, growing (24.5 s).
- **Archive.** Black-and-white footage for history (19.5-21 s).
- **Typography.** The reel's own is small white sans in the centre, with big bold words for the key ones. Ours replaces it (user): centred, small Helvetica over the big Instrument Serif glow.
