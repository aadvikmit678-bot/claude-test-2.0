## Rules for every segment

### Fonts

Only two, both upright:
- **Bold Helvetica-style sans** (Inter Tight Bold) wherever v4 used it. It stays as it is.
- **Instrument Serif, upright**, everywhere v4 used the slanted serif.

### Serif animation (from the "AI cold war" reference)

- **Small words in a line** ("that's why the", "The last time"): a glowing white box flashes for 2 frames where the word will sit, then the word writes on with a one-frame bloom. The words build up the line.
- **Hero words** ("AI", "timing", "Atomic Bomb"): no box. A blown-out white bloom for one frame, then crisp.
- **Every serif word** keeps a soft white halo.
- **Names** (the "Dario Amodei" treatment):
  - the first word blooms in with a corner light flare;
  - two glitch frames follow: the word goes dark, a cyan patch appears and a pale-yellow block marks where the second word lands;
  - the second word blooms in;
  - they leave word by word.
- **Transitions:** a horizontal white light streak can wipe between beats.

### Sans animation (from the F1 reference)

Each word blooms in centred, about 2 frames before it is spoken, and replaces the last one ("and / he'll be / the / first / to / tell / you").

### Black words on paper

They cut in at 114 % and settle over 0.4 s.

### Quality

- No full-frame shot is enlarged more than about 1.35×.
- Low-resolution sources only play as cards, panels or grid tiles, at or below their native size. The Kimmel clip in segment 1 is an example.

### Pace

The reference's rate: about 1–1.5 visual changes per second, and no hold over about 1.2 s except designed beats.
