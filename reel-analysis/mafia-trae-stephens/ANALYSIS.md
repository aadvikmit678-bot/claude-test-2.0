### Timing rules (measured)
- **The pictures follow the words, item by item**: each named thing gets its picture as it is said ("Silicon Valley"
  → Golden Gate, "Mafia" → MAFIA, "OpenAI" → Sam + the logo, "arming the Pentagon" → weapons, "poker player" → Liv).
- **Inside an item, the rhythm is 2 frames a picture** (flipbooks: Sam ×6, Palmer ×4+3 cards), **4–6 frames a shot**
  (the weapons montage), or one still for the punch (MAFIA, held 19 frames).
- **Punctuation frames** sit between items: 1-frame overexposure flash (27), 3-frame whip to white (59–61), 1-frame
  whip smears (81, 140, 276), colour negatives (115–117, 119, 367), a 1-frame logo (120), a diagonal slash wipe (228).
- **Captions are small and follow the pictures**: 1–2-word chunks that change on the cuts — on the word for the big
  ones (`12 of`, `MAFIA`, `open AI,`, the Trae reveal), otherwise up to ~0.5 s behind the voice.
- **The reveal gets the only build**: dip in the music (8.5–9.5 s), a "searching" device cycling candidates, a flash,
  the photo growing big, a bass boom (10.0–10.5 s), held 24 frames.
- **The hand-off to the story**: a single negative frame and a ~¼-s drop in the music, then the source clip's own sound.

### Type
1. **Captions** — white, bold, slightly rounded grotesque, small (~3 % of the height), centred at mid-height, with a soft
   white glow; 1–2 words at a time.
2. **Mixed accent** — in two-word chunks one word is a thin **italic serif** and the other the bold sans:
   *sat* **down**, *don't* **know**, *his* **name**, **Poker** *player.* (our Instrument Serif + Inter mix).
3. **Hero words** — the caption font at ~13 % of the height with a **speckled worn-print texture** inside the letters:
   `Silicon Valley`, `open AI,`.
4. **Title card** — `MAFIA` in a heavy red sans with a damask texture (the show's logo style), filling the frame.

### Look
Warm, punchy, vintage-print grade; a **curved old-screen** feel on the photos (barrel bulge, rounded dark corners,
vignette); the opening photo cards have **torn paper edges**; real footage from the game is kept clean.

### Sound
No click/hit track: the voice sits on a music bed that **swells** through "Silicon Valley… Mafia" (bass 25 → 44 dB),
carries a **noisy riser** under the weapons montage (6.6–8.1 s), **dips** before "And amongst them" (13–24 dB) and
**booms** on the reveal of Trae (46 dB at 10.0–10.5 s). See `img/opening_spectrogram.jpg`.

## 2. After the opening (15.35 – 70.9 s): 85 shots, median 11 frames (0.46 s)

Not locked to a beat — cut to the voice. Recurring tools: the game footage (with the show's lower thirds and its own
sound for the dialogue), **metaphor stills** for abstract words (an "analyst" with geometry sketches, grainy B&W noise
for "one problem", a Renaissance angel for "the angel", a macro eye for "pointing at", goosebumps for "the threat",
sepia maps and surveillance overlays for "intelligence… fake names", dollar bills for "$60 billion"), **proof clips**
(CNBC, Bloomberg, Stanford, FII), **green duotone cut-outs** of tech leaders for "Silicon Valley" (5–7 frames each), a
curved photo wall for "Everyone thought", the white light-beam card again ("one problem"), hero words (`IN FACT`,
`nobody`, `intelligence` black on white), and a **callback**: "He built the weapons company" replays the opening's
weapons montage shot for shot. Ends on cinematic eye close-ups and a white diamond light flare. Sheets: `img/body_shots_*.jpg`.

## 3. Why the opening works
1. **A room of names, each a picture**: the viewer recognises Sam Altman, Palmer Luckey, the poker champion — the
   status of the room is *shown*, not told, so the unknown name at the end lands as the twist.
2. **Fast inside, clear between**: 2-frame flipbooks and 4-frame montages give speed, but each item is separated by a
   flash/whip/negative, so it never turns to mush.
3. **One build for the protagonist**: the music dips, a selection box "searches" the room, a flash, and his photo
   grows — the only slow beat in the first 15 s.

## 4. Recipe: this opening for our next edit

Script shape: `[N] of the [most X] [people/minds] in [place] [did a thing]: the [one] behind [A], the [one] who [B],
even a [C]. And amongst them was [PROTAGONIST]. You probably don't know [his] name, but watch what [he] does.`

| Beat | Length | Device |
