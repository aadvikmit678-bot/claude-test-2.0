Built from `tools/edit.py` (cut, captions, transitions, sound) by `tools/prep.py` (clips, stills, audio) and `tools/build.py` (the HyperFrames project); `tools/mix.py` writes the delivered mix.

**What the user asked for, and where it is**
- The cold open as planned: the FOX 2 line "A video game so popular it could cost the U.S. economy a billion dollars in just one day" (0-6.5 s), static and a flash, CJ's "Ah shit, here we go again" (6.7-9.2 s), then the voiceover. Crazy type on the news: stacked serif pairs, a $1,000,000,000 counter landing on "dollars", "one day." in the highlight bar.
- Horizontal 16:9 throughout (vertical sources cut to a 16:9 band or carded).
- Fast cut, mostly GTA footage: trailers, the launch trailer, the Netflix gameplay and the fan montages from the folder, cut on the words (94 shots, the middle at ~0.5-0.8 s a shot); the named clips on their lines (took 13 years, millions, freedom, kid cinematic, escape from reality, adulthood rush, fast clip, gta 5 goodbye, metav3rse, the Vice City clips, panzer gameplay).
- No money footage anywhere (the counters carry "a billion dollars" and "2 billion dollars" as type over GTA shots).
- Instrument Serif glow at reel3's sizes (running 64 px, key 96, heroes 132-156), word by word on the spoken word, never static (the Tesla reel's drift), and the Dimon / Pattinson motion: the glowing square before each word, the hero bloom, the year inversion (2025, 2026, 19th, 13, 10), the highlight bar (one day., November., problem.), plus the Tesla reel's weight flash and light streaks. No bounce, no stretched type.
- Reel scenes: the Tesla reel's tile wall with the VI card ("when they play GTA 6"), its white card with black type ("But why?"), reel3's wall of faces ("Because GTA was never just about"), reel2's card with words either side ("GTA 6 took | 13 years"), a 2x2 grid that fills a tile a word ("everything you imagined adulthood would be"), the Miami / Vice City split.
- Motion graphics: the struck-out official date cards (COMING 2025, MAY 26 2026, then NOVEMBER 19 2026), the 2013 → 2026 year roller, the CRT family computer with Vice City on it, the TV volume bar falling 16 → 0, the notebook with ASPIRINE and PANZER written on the word and a Rhino polaroid.
- Slow where the script turns: "Freedom.", "But why?", "Take the long way home. Drive slow.", "never really left."; the ending on the VI logo and NOVEMBER 19, 2026.
