1440x1080 (4:3), 20 fps (a stepped, low-frame-rate look), 15.15 s (300 frames, every frame read on dense_00-02; full-size frames checked for the small text). Burned-in yellow subtitles at the bottom ("Unofficial fan-made GTA VI animation. All artwork sourced from rockstargames.com", then the lyric lines "You say", "but this pain in my", "it be.."). A PEGI "18 www.pegi.info" badge sits bottom right for the first 2.4 s.

## What it is
A "Vox explainer style" paper-collage animation built from the official GTA VI key art (the illustrated character panels, not trailer footage), cut to a sung track (lyrics on screen: "But this pain in my heart ... can't let it be"). Everything is a print / paper look: an off-white paper ground with a dark rough-edged film border (vignette), grain, flat cut-out illustrations that slide, tilt and stack; halftone dots; newspaper-headline type. Arc: blank paper -> a sliding panel layout of the key-art portraits (Lucia, Jason) -> the GTA VI cover collage -> the cover breaks apart -> a cut-out of Jason and Lucia in gold with palm trees over a car -> lyric type on the paper -> a newspaper pull-quote card "'CAN'T LET IT BE.'" -> postcards of Leonida places held by hands -> a zine collage (flamingo, licence plate, a character) -> the couple cut-out stacked on a car -> it slides off, black.

## Transitions & effects
- **Collage cut-outs on paper**: flat illustrations with white paper edges, layered, sliding and tilting a few pixels each frame (stepped, 20 fps).
- **Echo / repeat trail**: one cut-out repeated 6-8 times stepped behind itself (f126-145).
- **Torn paper wipes** (f92-97, f188-194) and **white bar stacks** (f37-40).
- **Logo break-apart**: the wordmark letters fly apart rotating (f59-61).
- **Stepped paper tunnel** push-in (f62-87).
- **Letterbox in** for a beat (f146-157).
- **Type-on lyric** in ransom-note blocks (word by word, f122-145) and a newspaper pull-quote that types on, then holds with red brush underlines.
- **Squeeze to a strip and slide off** as the ending (f255-299).
- Constant: a dark rough-edged film border and grain over everything.

## Typography
- Lyric blocks: condensed bold caps, white on black rectangles, each word its own block, slightly rotated (ransom-note / punk zine).
- Pull quote: condensed heavy sans caps ('CAN'T LET IT BE.") with curly quotes, white on a black tab with a cut corner; red label tab; serif body text below.
- Burned-in yellow subtitles at the bottom (small, sans).

## Rhythm
New layout every 0.6-1.2 s; inside each, something moves every frame at 20 fps (the stepped stop-motion feel). Postcards land one a beat.

## Look
Desaturated, tinted art (mauve, grey, purple, gold), off-white paper, heavy grain, dark vignette border: a printed-zine look.

## Steal for our edit
1. A collage section built from the official key art (cut-out characters on off-white paper) at 12-20 fps.

4. A newspaper pull-quote card for a key line of the voiceover.
5. Hands holding postcards of Leonida places (Vice City, Port Gellhorn, Grassrivers, Mount Kalaga, Ambrosia) for a "places" beat.
6. Torn-paper wipes; end by squeezing the page to a strip and sliding it off.
