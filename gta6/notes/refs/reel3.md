# reel3: Instagram reel DfEjvKjvRpz, "The most dangerous thing you can do to a founder is betray him." (font and caption-size reference)

1276x718, 30 fps, 65.27 s, 154 shots (median 0.27 s). Palmer Luckey / Oculus / Anduril story under a male voiceover, a dark cinematic score. Studied on full-frame-rate crops (frames 0-63, 628-657, 746-775), whole frames around the flash cuts (812-841, 900-929) and 12 stills. **This is the reference for the font and the caption size.**

## Font
A high-contrast condensed display serif, set tight, white with a soft white glow: the "Instrument Serif glow" look. Lower case, sentence case; no italics seen. Over light shots the type turns **black** ("a report came out" on a white search page, f648-657).

## Motion
- Word by word on the spoken word. **Each word arrives with a bloom**: frame 1 blown out (big bright halo, slightly soft), frame 2 half, frame 3 rest ("The" f0-2, "most" f9-10, "dangerous" f17-20, "thing" f27-28, "you" f35-36, "kid" f759, "started" f767).
- The block **slides with an ease-out**: "The kid who started the" moves left 30 px in its first 5 frames, then 17 px over 6, then 11 px over 8 (f755-774), drifting to rest.
- **Stacked two-line layout**: "The most" over "dangerous thing", the second line indented to the right and tucked up under the first (leading ~0.8, descenders touching ascenders). Same for "a report / came out".
- Captions often sit in dark gaps: black frames or a vignetted shot with only a small line of type ("last year", 47.6 s; "the guy who killed", f911-929).

## Transitions
- **Bloom flash cut**: an overexposed frame, a 1-frame negative (inverted) frame, a light-leak frame, then the new shot (f816-818, f835-837).
- **White bar wipe**: a soft-edged white band sweeps right to left across the frame and reveals the next shot (f905-910).
- **Double exposure**: two shots blended at ~50% for 2 frames before a cut (f814-815).
- **TV static**: 6 frames of digital noise, then dark (f636-646).
- Photo walls: a curved wall of portrait tiles with a slow camera move (0-3 s), floating photo cards over black (26 s).
- 16 flash events and 9 black holds (1-68 frames) in 65 s.

## What we take for the GTA edit
The font look and the sizes above (running ~56-72 px, key lines ~96 px, a few heroes ~130 px), the bloom-on-arrival, the ease-out slide, the stacked layout, black type on light shots, the flash cut (bloom + negative + leak) and the white bar wipe.
