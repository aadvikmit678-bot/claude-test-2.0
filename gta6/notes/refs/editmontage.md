# editmontage: "gta edit montage.mp4"

1400x1080 (about 1.3:1), 60 fps, 12.12 s (720 frames, every second frame read on dense_00-03). Music only. The tightest beat sync in the folder: 23 of 24 cuts fall within one frame of a music onset, every ~0.47 s (one cut a beat at ~128 BPM).

## What it is
A clean GTA VI trailer montage whose transitions are mostly **split-screen pushes and card drops** rather than flashes: the next shot enters as a vertical half or a tilted photo card over the current one, then takes the frame. Arc: Lucia in the prison yard -> Lucia close-up -> a man on the phone -> a motorbike on a roof -> tilted cards (Lucia at a window, a fighter, the official Jason-and-Lucia key art) -> Jason and Lucia by a car -> masked robbers -> a woman on a balcony at sunset -> a couple, a man at night, the club, a dancer, Lucia in a car, Jason at night with fireworks, a tattooed man, the prison again, a swamp at sunset, the gang at night, a car interior -> a picture-in-picture shrinks to the top left, black.

## Transitions & effects
- **Vertical split-screen entry** (f29, f268, f282, f382, f394): the incoming shot appears as one half of the frame for 15-30 frames, then takes the whole frame. Halves can swap sides.
- **Tilted photo cards** dropping in one after another (f156, f170, f184), slightly rotated, each a beat.
- **Picture-in-picture** card that grows to full (f337-366).
- **Porthole / fisheye vignette** on the opening and closing prison shots.
- **Shrink-to-corner exit** (f674) into black.
- Otherwise straight cuts on the beat.

## Typography
None.

## Rhythm
One change a beat (~0.47 s at ~128 BPM); the card run is faster (~0.23 s each, half beats); the last run speeds up (0.2-0.25 s shots, f590-673).

## Look
Trailer grade; a soft, clean look (no grain or flashes).

## Steal for our edit
1. Split-screen entries: the next shot takes half the frame for half a beat, then the whole frame.
2. A run of tilted photo cards, one a half-beat, ending on the official key art.
3. PiP card that grows to full frame.
4. Bookend with the same shot (the prison yard) at the start and near the end.
5. Exit by shrinking the frame into a corner and cutting to black.
6. Cut exactly on the beat (this one lands 23 of 24 cuts within a frame).
