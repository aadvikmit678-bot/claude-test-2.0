## Transitions & effects
- **2-frame white flash on the cut** (f242-243, 275-276, 395-396, 463-464, 528-529, 593-594): the main device, nearly every beat. At 60 fps that is 33 ms of pure white.
- **Half-frame split wipes**: vertical (f199-204, f303-307, f427-429) and horizontal (f493-494): for 2-5 frames the frame is two halves, the new shot entering on one side, often with a white panel.
- **Silhouette matte flash**: a moving person filled solid white (f269-270, f388-389) or black (f273) for 1-2 frames: a mask of the subject flashed in.
- **X light-streak transition** (f351-356): two diagonal streaks with chromatic fringing cross, a white frame, the X lingers on the next shot.
- **Radial spin-zoom** into a whirl (f555-560).
- **Vertical light streak / leak** opening into white (f590-594).
- **Fade through white** (f659-662), used once, for the slower ending.
- **Drain to black and white** over ~6 frames before a cut (f236-241).
- **Barrel / fisheye warp** with curved black edges on one shot (f277-302).

## Look
Native trailer grade (warm sunsets, purple / magenta night neon), with occasional desaturation and high-contrast monochrome shots; the white flashes give the whole thing a strobing, bright feel.

## Steal for our edit
1. Open on the R* logo on black (fade in ~0.4 s, hold), close on the VI logo.
2. One shot a beat (~0.55 s) with a 2-frame white flash on the cut.
3. Mix in half-frame split wipes (vertical and horizontal, 3-5 frames) instead of every flash.
4. Silhouette flash: fill the running subject white for 1-2 frames right before the cut.
5. An X light-streak or a light-leak streak as a special transition, once or twice.
6. Drain a shot to black and white in its last 6 frames before a flash.
7. End slower: two ~1.1 s holds (sunset skyline, nature) joined by a 4-frame fade through white, then the logo.
