## Caveats
- Low resolution: kidcine, fastclip, klickpin (736x414), adultrush (702x396), escape (720x360), millions (720x780). Upscaled 2.5-3x they go soft; keep them short, behind grain and a grade, or in a card.
- Burned-in text: took13 (its graphics), metav3rse (caption and subtitles), gta5bye (the years), vcpanzer (HUD and the note), vc10 (ESRB, cards).
- Music and dialogue in the clips are muted in our edit except where the edit uses a clip's own line.
