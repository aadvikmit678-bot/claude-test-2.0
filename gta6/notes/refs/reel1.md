# reel1: Instagram reel DezFTyktEUp, the Tesla Roadster reel (typography motion reference)

1280x720, 23.976 fps, 58.05 s, 113 shots (median 0.42 s; 77 of 112 cuts on a music onset), music at ~82 BPM under a male voiceover ("So the world finally has 'the flying car.' ... It just has to send people to the car that does."). "PRODUCED BY LUMITRIS" bug top right. Studied on full-frame-rate crops of the caption band (frames 0-63, 175-206, 318-349) and 12 stills across the film. **This is the reference for how the captions move.**

## The caption system, measured
| what | how (frames at 23.976 fps) |
|---|---|
| font | a neo-grotesque (Helvetica Neue / SF-like): Medium for normal words, Heavy/Black only as an entrance frame |
| colour | white with a soft white bloom (halo ~10-20 px at 720p); **black on white or very light shots** (f175-206 "But there's a slight misunderstanding." on a white frame) |
| reveal | one word at a time, **on the spoken word** (within ±2 frames of its onset: "Last" 13.22 s → f318, "week" → f324, "Tesla's" → f329, "new" → f341, "Roadster" → f345) |
| layout | the whole line is laid out first, centred on its final width; words appear **in their final places** (no reflow). Normal lines use very wide word gaps (~3 spaces); emphasis lines are tight (negative tracking) |
| hold | **the line never stands still**: the whole block drifts left at a constant ~0.6-1.3 px/frame at 720p (≈20-45 px/s at 1080p), from the first word to the cut |
| exit | dies with the shot (hard cut), or a grey card / white panel wipes across it (f57-59, f326-328) and the next phrase starts on the new shot |

## Word entrances (four kinds, mixed through the film)
1. **Plain pop**: the word is simply there on its frame (e.g. "week" f324, "new" f341, "the" f4).
2. **Scale pop**: frame 1 at ~1.5-1.6x and brighter, frame 2 at 1.0 ("Last" f318→f319; "world" f7 at 1.2x).
3. **Weight flash** (on key words and every emphasis phrase): frames 1-2 in a heavy weight with a strong bloom, frame 2 smeared sideways (motion blur), frame 3 the normal weight. "the" f33-35, "flying" f39-41, "car" f48-50, "Tesla's" f329-331, "Roadster" f345-347, "misunderstanding." f193-195 (in black, on white).
4. **Light streaks**: 1-2 frames of vertical white streaks filling the word's box, then the clean word ("world" f8, "finally" f14-15).

## Other moves
- Emphasis phrases replace the running line and sit centred, bigger and tighter: "the flying car", "Tesla Roadster", "misunderstanding.", "Millions".
- 1-frame inverted (negative) frames and 1-frame white frames as cut accents (f2, shot 9 at 3.92 s, shot 14 at 6.22 s).
- A grid of tiles / a Tesla logo card (6.2 s), light-streak overlays, B&W shots mixed with colour.

## What we take for the GTA edit
Word-on-the-word reveals, words appearing in their final places, the never-still drift, the four entrance kinds (in Instrument Serif the weight flash becomes a stroke-thickened, blurred, over-bright frame), black type on light shots, captions dying with the shot.
